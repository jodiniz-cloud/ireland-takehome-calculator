About This Calculator

Last updated: July 2026

IEPayCalc.io is a free, independent tool that estimates take-home pay in Ireland after income tax, USC, and PRSI. It is not affiliated with Revenue, the Department of Social Protection, any employer, or any bank or financial institution — it exists to give a quick, transparent estimate before or during salary negotiations.

How the Calculation Works

The tool applies the 2025 standard and higher income tax rates (20% and 40%) against the relevant rate band for your status — single, married with one income, or married with two incomes — then subtracts the standard Personal and Employee tax credits (€1,875 each). It separately calculates USC across its four bands (0.5%, 2%, 4%, 8%) and Class A PRSI at 4% above the weekly threshold, matching the structure Revenue uses for most PAYE employees.

What This Calculator Does Not Do

It does not model emergency tax, benefit-in-kind (such as a company car), share-based remuneration, additional credits like the Home Carer or Medical Expenses credit, or self-employed/Class S PRSI. Occupational pension contributions are handled as a simple percentage deducted before tax and USC, which is accurate for standard schemes but does not reflect age-related contribution limits. Every figure here is an estimate, not a substitute for a payslip, a Revenue statement, or advice from a registered tax agent or accountant.

No Affiliation

This site has no connection to Revenue, the Department of Social Protection, or any bank. For official figures or a personalised tax review, use Revenue's myAccount service at revenue.ie or consult a registered tax professional.
